About me

Born in Ulm, Germany, in 1879, I have spent my life asking simple questions about light, time and space. I received the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics for explaining the photoelectric effect. I believe curiosity matters more than having every answer, and I am happy to be learning about today's world.
